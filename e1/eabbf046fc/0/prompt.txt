## Changes

- Keep all columns, indexes, just drop the UNIQUE constraint

- **POST `/:id/reports`** (~line 290): Remove the `catch` block that handles UNIQUE constraint violations (the 409 response with `existing_report`). Without the constraint, inserts will just succeed.

- **`handleBezirkSubmit`** (~line 190): Remove the inner try/catch that catches 409 and patches existing report. Just do the POST — it will always create a new report now.

### 4. Client: `client/src/components/LandMode.tsx`
- **`handleBezirkSubmit`** (~line 131): Same removal — remove the inner try/catch 409 handling.

### 5. Stats query: `server/src/routes/exercises.ts`
